How a roadside tyre change works

From your call to back on the road.

Scroll through the six steps — the scene follows along.

Step 1 Call us

  1. 1

    Call us or WhatsApp

    A real person answers, 24/7. Give your tyre size or registration and where you are. Price and ETA are confirmed before we dispatch.

  2. 2

    Van dispatched

    The nearest fitter heads straight to your home, workplace or roadside spot — 30 minutes on average, often 20.

  3. 3

    Car lifted safely

    We fit on-site, so there’s no driving on a damaged tyre, no tow wait and no garage queue.

  4. 4

    Old tyre off

    Wheel nuts come off and the damaged tyre is removed. If it’s repairable we repair it on the spot; otherwise we replace it.

  5. 5

    New tyre on, nuts torqued

    New tyre fitted with a new valve and balancing, wheel nuts torqued correctly and pressure set to manufacturer spec.

  6. 6

    Back on the road

    The old tyre is disposed of responsibly and you drive away. Same service at 3am as at 3pm.

About K1

15 years of showing up at 2am.

K1 Dover Tyres was founded in 2010 by Mr Hossain Hamedi in Dover — a port town where drivers need help at 2am. Our goal has always been simple: be there for the people of Kent when they need us most, any hour of the day or night.

Today a fleet of 10+ fully stocked vans and 10+ qualified technicians handle 200+ jobs a week. We set pressures to manufacturer spec, torque wheels correctly and dispose of old tyres responsibly.
