About K1 Dover Tyres

15 years of being there when Kent needs us

K1 Dover Tyres was founded in 2010 by Mr Hossain Hamedi. Dover is a port town where drivers can need help at 2am, so the goal has always been simple: be there for the people of Kent when they need us most, any hour of the day or night. Not a service that closes at 5pm or charges excessive call-out fees.

  • 10+ fully stocked vans and 10+ qualified technicians
  • Pressures set to manufacturer spec and wheels torqued correctly
  • Old tyres disposed of responsibly
  • A real person answers, a fixed price before we start, the same service at 3am as 3pm

Base: Beaconsfield Rd, Buckland, Dover, CT16 2LL. Most of our work is mobile.
