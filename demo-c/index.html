What we do

Nine ways we get you back on the road

Everything comes to you. Call or WhatsApp, we confirm a fixed price and send a van.

  • Mobile Tyre Fitting

    New tyres fitted at home, work or roadside, with no garage queue. All brands from budget to premium. Emergency and roadside call-outs included.

  • Mobile Tyre Repair

    Nail in the tyre or a slow puncture? We assess and repair on the spot, no towing. Emergency and roadside repairs too.

  • Mobile Tyre Replacement

    Blowout or worn out? Tyres supplied and fitted on site, wherever you are stuck, day or night.

  • Jump Start Services

    Flat battery? A fitter arrives with a professional booster pack for petrol, diesel and start-stop cars, plus a basic battery check.

  • Part Worn Tyres

    Legal, inspected part-worn tyres: marked, pressure tested and checked to BS AU 159. See the towns we cover below.

  • Battery Replacement

    Car will not start? We test on site and fit the right battery for your car, van or hybrid, usually the same day.

  • Breakdown Recovery

    Flatbed and wheel-lift recovery, 24/7. We try a roadside fix first so you are not recovered unnecessarily.

  • Locking Wheel Nut Removal

    Lost the key, or a nut is rounded or broken? Controlled extraction designed to protect your wheel studs.

  • Fuel Delivery

    Ran dry? Emergency petrol or diesel brought to the roadside, home, work or car park. Price and ETA confirmed first.

    Since 2010

    A Dover family business that picks up at 2am

    K1 Dover Tyres was founded in 2010 by Mr Hossain Hamedi. Dover is a port town, and drivers here need help at all hours. Hossain wanted to build the sort of service he would want to call himself: one that does not close at five and does not charge excessive call-out fees.

    Fifteen years on, the fleet is 10+ fully stocked vans and 10+ qualified technicians, covering around 200 jobs a week. We set pressures to the manufacturer's spec, torque wheels correctly and dispose of old tyres responsibly.
